Biodiesel and Soap Manufacturing Startups which take used cooking oil from food business operators, to comply with registration and further orders and notifications

Biodiesel and soap manufacturing startups using Used Cooking Oil (UCO) from Food Business Operators must comply with FSSAI’s registration requirements, as per the order dated 5th August 2024. Accurate submission of information is crucial, and failure to do so may result in the cancellation of their enrollment, impacting their operations in the FMCG sector.
