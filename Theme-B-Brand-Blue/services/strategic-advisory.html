Strategic Advisory

Organizations might have the most powerful employees & innovative technology but what is essential for the most impactful business value in these times of perpetual state of change with emerging technologies and shift in customer behavior is the right strategic advisory. Strategic Advisory ensures that businesses pull out maximum value from their current systems, avoid potential technology pitfalls and attain the highest return possible.

At Ab Initio our team of senior consultant, provide strategic advisory from large to mid-sized business. We provide executive-level guidance in managing key risk areas, optimizing the value of technology, and introducing innovation to your business in cost-effective measures by constantly reviewing potential risks and opportunities that will improve business operations. We use strategic planning analysis, modeling tools, business valuations, and financial capacity among others to make strategies to support & stimulate your business. We at Ab Initio address the critical stages in the corporate development and business transaction lifecycle, from planning and financial analysis to transaction execution and ongoing monitoring of transaction value. Our strategic advisory services fully understand the financial objectives and long-term goals of our business and make optimal strategies to make a strong execution plan. We first introspect your business at present then we conduct a deep study into the prevailing business environment. We further compare your present strategies with the prevailing market conditions. Then we draft a strategy plan of setting and overall strategic direction. We then further make plans to execute the strategies.

We provide the following strategic advisory services:

  • Capital raising
  • Fairness opinions
  • Corporate governance
  • Strategic alternatives advisory
  • Corporate development advisory
  • Special committee financial advisory
  • Prioritization of shareholder objectives
  • Acquisitions, joint ventures, and alliances
  • Sale and divestiture planning and advisory
