Ab Initio uses cookies for improving and customizing users’ experiences. Cookies are a piece of a data which is sent by the website and stored in the user web browser. It helps the website to load the user’s previous activity anytime when the website is reloaded. Ab Initio uses cookies to keep track of user’s activity so that it can recall your given information and preferences, every time when you load the website thereby making your use of the website faster and convenient. Cookies do not carry viruses nor install malware on the host computer. However, the use of cookies is optional and it is upon the discretion of the user to accept or decline cookies. User may also change their browser setting to decline all or any of the cookies. Accepting the cookie is not essential for accessing this website, but declining cookies may prevent you from accessing some features of the website thereby restricting your use of the site.
