Separate Bank Account Required for Maharashtra RERA Projects Registered After 1st July 2024

The Maharashtra RERA order dated 1st July 2024 requires promoters of newly registered real estate projects to maintain a separate bank account for each project. This measure ensures that 70% of the funds collected from allottees are exclusively used for the specific project, enhancing transparency and financial discipline. Non-compliance could result in penalties and legal consequences.
