Our team at Ab Initio India LLP understands that every situation is unique. However, there are some common steps in every business advisory process that we follow for a systematic approach:

  1. Detailed discussion to understand your business advisory needs.
  2. Reviewing your existing business plan (draft business plan), the financial position of your business, market research, competitor research, future plans, etc.
  3. Follow-up meetings to discuss details, services fees, timeline, and final deliverables.
  4. Further, we have follow-up meetings for a better understanding of the revenue and expense models of your business to make the best suited overall financial model furtherance of discussions and development.
  5. Draft necessary presentations, further research, executive summaries, etc, and any other document as required by the client. Each document prepared will be thoroughly discussed and reviewed regularly.
  6. Prepare a draft outline of the business plan and discuss feedback and incorporate the necessary changes.
  7. Initiating strategy implementation.
  8. Coach the client and continue providing ongoing support ranging from minor to major updates.
  9. Discuss the feedback on the implementation of the business plan.

Setting up business in India involves the following steps:

  1. Understanding the client’s interest to start up a business in India.
  2. Setting up detailed meetings for briefing about the potential business ideas around the interest of the client.
  3. Discussing suitable business entry for client’s idea (Proprietorship, Partnership, LLP, Pvt. Ltd., OPC, PUB LTD, etc.)
  4. Formulating a suitable execution plan.
  5. Incorporation of business.
  6. Applying and obtaining industry-specific licenses and registrations.
  7. Applying and obtaining revenue law and labor law registrations.
  8. Opening bank accounts for business.
  9. Preparing a draft outline of the business plan and discuss feedback and incorporate the necessary changes.
  10. Initiating strategy implementation.
  11. Coach the client and continue providing ongoing support ranging from minor to major updates.
  12. Discuss the feedback on the implementation of the business plan.

In general, there is no pre-determined fixed fee for our business advisory services. The service fee is determined by:

  1. The scope of the project, in terms of the tasks and documents, the client requires help with.
  2. The quality of the client’s existing materials, including drafts of business plans, financial forecasts, market research, competitive research, etc.
  3. The vision of the client’s business model, marketing and distribution strategy, financial plans, etc.
  4. The complexity of the client’s industry and business model.
  5. The availability of industry information.
  6. The desired timing relative to Ab Initio India LLP workload. In general, “rush jobs” or “priority jobs” will carry a substantial premium.
